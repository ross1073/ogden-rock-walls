## Retaining Wall Services in Washington Terrace

Washington Terrace is a small residential community nestled between Ogden and South Ogden, with neighborhoods built primarily in the post-World War II era through the 1970s. The community sits on gently rolling terrain with moderate grade changes — enough to require retaining walls, but not the dramatic slopes of the east bench.

The defining retaining wall challenge in Washington Terrace is age. Many of the community's walls are 40 to 60 years old and were built with materials and methods that don't meet current standards.

## Aging Wall Replacement

Washington Terrace has one of the highest concentrations of aging retaining walls in Weber County. These structures — poured concrete without rebar, dry-stacked block, railroad ties, and other outdated materials — are failing throughout the community.

Common failure signs we see in Washington Terrace include walls leaning away from the retained soil, horizontal cracks along mortar joints, soil washing out from behind walls during rain, and walls that have settled several inches below their original position.

We specialize in removing these old walls and replacing them with properly engineered structures that include modern drainage systems.

## Working in Tight Spaces

Washington Terrace lots are typical of mid-century residential subdivisions — moderate size with homes relatively close together. Retaining wall work here often requires operating equipment in tight spaces between houses, around fences, and near utility lines.

We operate compact equipment specifically suited for this kind of work. Our smaller excavators and skid steers can access tight residential lots while still having the power to excavate, demolish old walls, and place new materials.
