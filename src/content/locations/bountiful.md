## Retaining Wall Services in Bountiful

Bountiful's east bench is one of the most desirable residential areas in Davis County — and one of the most challenging for retaining wall construction. Properties along the hillside from 400 North to Pages Lane climb steeply toward the Sessions Mountains, with panoramic valley views that come at the cost of serious terrain management.

Retaining walls on Bountiful's bench aren't optional — they're essential infrastructure that makes these hillside properties functional and safe.

## East Bench Hillside Properties

The grade changes on Bountiful's east bench rival anything in Weber or Davis County. Lots routinely have 20 to 40 feet of elevation change, requiring substantial retaining structures to create usable outdoor space. Many properties need walls on multiple sides, often combined with terracing to break the slope into manageable levels.

We design wall systems for these properties that balance structural requirements with aesthetics. Boulder walls blend naturally with the mountain landscape, while block walls offer clean lines for more formal outdoor living spaces.

## Sessions Mountains and Rockfall

Properties closest to the Sessions Mountains face additional considerations. Natural rockfall from above, shallow bedrock, and steep natural drainage channels all affect retaining wall design and placement. We assess these hazards during our initial site visit and design walls that account for conditions above the property, not just on it.
