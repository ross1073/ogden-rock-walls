## Retaining Wall Services in Roy

Roy sits on the flatter terrain west of Ogden, where the ancient Lake Bonneville lakebed created wide, gently sloping ground. While Roy doesn't have the dramatic hillside slopes of the east bench communities, it has its own set of challenges that make retaining walls and drainage solutions important for property owners.

The high water table in western Roy, combined with clay-silt soil that drains poorly, means that water management is the primary concern for most retaining wall projects here.

## Water Table and Drainage Challenges

Parts of Roy — particularly neighborhoods west of 1900 West — sit on land with a high seasonal water table. During spring snowmelt and wet periods, groundwater can rise close to the surface, saturating soil and creating pressure against any below-grade retaining structure.

Retaining walls in these areas need robust drainage systems designed for high water conditions. We install French drain systems with proper slope and outlet points, and we use clean drain rock backfill that allows water to move freely rather than building up pressure.

## Grade Changes Around Roy

While Roy is relatively flat compared to North Ogden or Bountiful, many properties still have grade changes that require retaining walls. Common situations include elevation differences between neighboring lots, drainage swales and channels that need structural banks, and landscape terracing to create usable outdoor spaces on gently sloping lots.

Newer subdivisions in Roy often have minimal grading — builders cut and fill just enough to set foundations, leaving homeowners to deal with drainage and grade issues after moving in. We handle these post-construction grading and wall projects regularly.

## What We Build in Roy

Most Roy projects involve smaller retaining walls — two to four feet — combined with significant drainage work. Block walls are popular here for their clean appearance, and we always install drainage systems sized for Roy's high water table conditions. Contact us for a free estimate.
