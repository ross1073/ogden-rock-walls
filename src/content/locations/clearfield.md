## Retaining Wall Services in Clearfield

Clearfield occupies flat to gently sloping terrain in central Davis County, adjacent to Hill Air Force Base. The soil here is characteristic of the ancient Lake Bonneville lake bottom — fine-grained silts and clays that present specific challenges for retaining wall construction.

While Clearfield doesn't have the steep slopes of east bench communities, retaining walls are commonly needed for managing grade differences between properties, creating level landscape areas, and addressing drainage issues caused by the area's poor-draining soil.

## Lakebed Soil Challenges

The soil in Clearfield is predominantly silt and clay deposited when Lake Bonneville covered this valley. These soils are soft, compress under load, and drain very slowly. Retaining walls built on this soil type can settle unevenly if the base isn't properly prepared and compacted.

We prepare wall foundations in Clearfield by excavating to stable ground, compacting in controlled lifts, and sometimes using geotextile fabric to distribute loads across the soft subgrade. This prevents the differential settling that causes walls to crack and lean over time.

## Water Management

Clearfield's flat terrain and poor-draining soil mean that water tends to sit rather than flow away. Properties here commonly have wet spots, standing water after rain, and soggy soil conditions that affect retaining walls and foundations.

Our approach in Clearfield often combines retaining wall construction with comprehensive drainage systems — French drains behind walls, surface grading to direct water to collection points, and underground pipe systems to carry water to appropriate discharge locations.
