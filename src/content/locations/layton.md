## Retaining Wall Services in Layton

Layton stretches from the flatlands near Hill Air Force Base up to the steep Wasatch foothills east of Highway 89. The east bench neighborhoods — from Layton Hills Mall eastward toward the mountains — sit on some of the steepest residential terrain in Davis County.

These bench properties commonly need retaining walls to manage the natural grade, create usable yard space, and prevent erosion. The clay-heavy soil throughout Layton makes proper drainage critical for any retaining wall project.

## Clay Soil and Drainage

Layton's soil is predominantly clay, deposited when ancient Lake Bonneville covered this area. Clay soil is the worst possible backfill behind a retaining wall — it holds water, expands when wet, and creates enormous hydrostatic pressure. Many wall failures we repair in Layton trace back to builders who used native clay soil as backfill instead of clean drain rock.

Every wall we build in Layton includes a French drain system with filter fabric, perforated pipe, and clean angular drain rock. We remove the clay from behind the wall and replace it with material that allows water to drain freely.

## Kays Creek and East Bench Drainage

Properties along Kays Creek and the surrounding east bench neighborhoods deal with seasonal water flows that affect slope stability. Spring snowmelt sends water through these areas, saturating hillsides and increasing the load on retaining walls.

We design walls in these areas with oversized drainage capacity to handle peak water flow during spring runoff. This protects the wall during the most demanding months of the year.
