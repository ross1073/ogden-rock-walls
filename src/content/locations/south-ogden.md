## Retaining Wall Services in South Ogden

South Ogden's hillside neighborhoods between 40th Street and the mouth of Ogden Canyon contain some of the most challenging residential terrain in Weber County. Many homes were built in the 1960s through 1980s when retaining wall standards were less rigorous, and those aging walls are now reaching the end of their lifespan.

We see a high volume of repair and replacement work in South Ogden. Walls that have been holding for decades are starting to lean, crack, and fail as drainage systems clog and soil conditions shift over time.

## The Aging Wall Problem

South Ogden has a concentration of retaining walls built 30 to 50 years ago. Many of these walls were constructed with materials and methods that wouldn't pass current engineering standards — railroad ties, dry-stacked concrete block without reinforcement, or poured concrete walls with no drainage provisions.

These walls have served their time, but they're failing now because the wood has rotted, the unreinforced block can't handle the ongoing soil pressure, or decades of water infiltration have undermined their foundations. We specialize in assessing these aging walls and providing replacement solutions that will last another 50 years.

## Hillside Properties Along the Bench

The residential streets climbing east toward the Ogden Canyon mouth sit on steep terrain. Properties along Harrison Boulevard, 40th Street, and the surrounding neighborhoods often have retaining walls on multiple sides — front yard, back yard, and between neighbors.

When one of these walls starts to fail, it can affect neighboring properties. We work with homeowners to design replacement walls that solve the structural problem while respecting property lines and neighborhood aesthetics.

## Our Approach in South Ogden

We start every South Ogden project with a thorough assessment of the existing conditions. For replacement projects, we evaluate whether the old wall's foundation can be reused or needs complete removal. We always address drainage as part of the rebuild. Call for a free assessment.
