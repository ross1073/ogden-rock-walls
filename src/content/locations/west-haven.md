## Retaining Wall Services in West Haven

West Haven stretches across flat terrain west of Ogden where the Weber River meets its floodplain. The area's defining characteristic for construction is water — a high water table, saturated soil, and poor natural drainage that affects every structure built on or in the ground.

Retaining walls in West Haven face different challenges than hillside communities. Here, the problem isn't steep slopes — it's building stable structures in ground that's perpetually wet.

## High Water Table

West Haven's water table is among the highest in Weber County. In many areas, groundwater sits within a few feet of the surface year-round, and during wet seasons it can rise to within inches. Any retaining wall with a below-grade foundation is essentially sitting in water for part of the year.

We design walls for West Haven with this reality in mind — waterproof materials, drainage systems that manage groundwater rather than just surface water, and foundations sized for the reduced bearing capacity of saturated soil.

## Soft Soil Construction

The soil in West Haven is soft silt and clay that compresses under load. Standard wall foundations can settle unevenly, causing walls to crack and lean. We address this by overexcavating the foundation, compacting in controlled lifts with appropriate material, and in some cases using geogrid reinforcement to distribute loads across the soft subgrade.

## Drainage Is Everything

On West Haven's flat terrain, water doesn't flow away on its own. Every retaining wall project here includes a comprehensive drainage plan — not just behind the wall, but across the property. We grade surfaces to direct water to collection points and install underground drainage systems that carry water to appropriate outlets.
