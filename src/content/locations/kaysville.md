## East Bench Slopes

Kaysville's bench properties have the same steep terrain and clay soil conditions found throughout the Wasatch Front. Slopes that face east catch morning sun and dry out faster, while west-facing slopes stay wet longer and are more prone to saturation-related failures.

We account for slope orientation when designing drainage systems — west-facing walls in Kaysville need more drainage capacity than east-facing walls on the same property.
