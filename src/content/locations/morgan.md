## Retaining Wall Services in Morgan

Morgan sits in a mountain valley along the Weber River, east of the Wasatch Range through Weber Canyon. The terrain here is fundamentally different from the valley communities west of the mountains — steeper natural slopes, more variable soil conditions, and the constant influence of the Weber River on properties along its course.

Retaining wall projects in Morgan often involve larger scale work on rural properties, riverbank stabilization, and slope management on mountain terrain.

## Weber River Properties

Properties along the Weber River in Morgan deal with bank erosion, seasonal flooding, and soil that's been repeatedly saturated by high water events. Retaining walls near the river need to withstand occasional submersion, resist undermining from moving water, and hold back soil that may be perpetually wet.

We use heavy boulder walls for riverbank applications in Morgan — the mass and weight of large rocks resists water forces that would move lighter wall systems. Proper toe protection at the base of the wall prevents undermining during high water events.

## Mountain Terrain

The slopes above the Morgan valley floor climb into mountain terrain with thin soil over bedrock, natural rock outcrops, and steep grades. Retaining walls on these properties often require excavation through rock, which is standard work for our equipment and operators.

Access can be challenging on mountain properties. We assess equipment access as part of every Morgan project estimate, and we have machines of various sizes to handle both open rural lots and tight canyon properties.
