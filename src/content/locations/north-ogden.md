## Retaining Wall Services in North Ogden

North Ogden sits against some of the steepest residential terrain along the Wasatch Front. Properties on the east bench — from Pleasant View north through the foothills above 2600 North — deal with significant grade changes that make retaining walls a necessity rather than an option.

The combination of steep slopes, clay-heavy soil, and heavy spring snowmelt creates conditions where retaining walls take serious abuse. Walls built without proper drainage fail regularly here because the clay soil traps water and creates massive hydrostatic pressure during wet months.

## Local Challenges We Understand

North Ogden's bench communities sit on soil deposited by ancient Lake Bonneville. This soil is dense, clay-rich, and behaves unpredictably when it gets wet — expanding and pushing against retaining structures, then shrinking and settling when it dries. Walls that work fine in sandy soil often fail within a few years here.

The elevation changes across North Ogden properties can be dramatic. Lots along the east bench frequently have 10 to 20 feet of grade change from front to back. These slopes need structural boulder walls or engineered block wall systems — not landscape timbers or stacked stone.

We also see heavy spring runoff from Ben Lomond Peak and the surrounding mountains. Water flows downhill through North Ogden neighborhoods and saturates slopes that were stable during dry months. Proper drainage behind retaining walls is critical here.
