## Retaining Wall Services in Eden

Eden sits in the heart of Ogden Valley, surrounded by the mountains that draw skiers to Powder Mountain and Nordic Valley. The mountain terrain, seasonal ground movement, and heavy snowfall create retaining wall challenges distinct from the valley communities below.

Properties in Eden range from valley-floor lots near the commercial center to steep mountain homesites with dramatic elevation changes. Both need retaining solutions designed for Eden's specific conditions.

## Mountain Ground Movement

Eden's elevation — roughly 5,000 feet and higher — means the ground freezes deeper and stays frozen longer than the valley below. This freeze-thaw cycling causes the ground to heave in winter and settle in spring, putting repeated stress on retaining walls. Walls built without accounting for frost depth can shift and crack within a few seasons.

We build walls in Eden with foundations set below the frost line and flexible enough to accommodate seasonal ground movement. Boulder walls are particularly effective here because individual rocks can shift slightly without compromising the overall structure.

## Spring Snowmelt

Ogden Valley receives significantly more snow than the Ogden valley floor. When that snow melts in spring, the ground becomes saturated for weeks. Retaining walls need drainage systems that can handle this sustained water load — not just occasional rain events, but weeks of continuous groundwater flow.

We size drainage systems in Eden for peak snowmelt conditions, with larger pipe and more drain rock than we'd use on a comparable wall at lower elevation.

## Rocky Terrain

Eden's soil often includes significant rock — from small cobble to large boulders embedded in the ground. Excavation for wall foundations requires heavy equipment and experienced operators who know how to work efficiently in rocky conditions. As an excavation company, this is our core capability.
