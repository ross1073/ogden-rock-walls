## Built to Hold — Not Just to Look Good

Rock and boulder retaining walls are the strongest option for holding back earth on steep slopes across the Wasatch Front. Unlike decorative garden walls, these structures use massive, interlocking boulders weighing thousands of pounds each — placed with precision using heavy excavation equipment.

At Ogden Rock Walls, we specialize in structural boulder walls that solve real problems: hillside erosion, failing slopes, unusable backyards, and property damage from shifting soil. Every wall we build starts with proper site assessment, engineered drainage, and compacted base preparation.

## Our Process

We don't just stack rocks. Our installation process follows a proven sequence that ensures long-term stability:

1. **Site assessment** — We evaluate slope grade, soil conditions, water flow patterns, and access for equipment.
2. **Excavation and base prep** — We dig to stable ground, compact the subgrade, and install drainage behind the wall before a single boulder is placed.
3. **Boulder selection and placement** — Each rock is chosen for structural fit, then set with excavators to create an interlocking, gravity-based wall system.
4. **Backfill and drainage** — Clean drain rock and perforated pipe go behind every wall to prevent the hydrostatic pressure that causes failures.
5. **Finish grading** — We grade the surrounding area for proper water runoff away from the wall.

## When You Need Boulder Walls

Boulder retaining walls are the right choice when you're dealing with steep grade changes, heavy soil loads, or large-scale slope stabilization. They're common across Northern Utah properties built against the Wasatch Range foothills, where natural terrain drops can exceed 30 degrees.

If your property has a slope that needs more than four feet of retention, boulder walls deliver the mass and structural integrity that block walls can't match at that scale.
