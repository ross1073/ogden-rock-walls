## Fixing Walls That Are Failing

A retaining wall that's leaning, cracking, or bulging isn't just an eyesore — it's a structural failure in progress. Left alone, a failing wall will eventually collapse, potentially damaging property, landscaping, and even structures downhill.

At Ogden Rock Walls, we specialize in diagnosing why walls fail and fixing the root cause — not just patching the symptoms. Most wall failures we see across the Ogden area come down to one of three problems: inadequate drainage, poor base preparation, or undersized materials for the soil load.

## Common Signs Your Wall Needs Repair

If you notice any of these issues, your retaining wall needs professional attention:

- **Leaning or tilting** away from the retained soil
- **Horizontal cracks** along mortar joints or block faces
- **Bulging sections** where hydrostatic pressure is building
- **Soil washing out** from behind or underneath the wall
- **Separation** at corners or where the wall meets other structures
- **Settlement** or sinking of the wall into the ground

## How We Approach Repairs

Every repair project starts with a thorough assessment. We determine whether the wall can be stabilized in place or needs partial or full reconstruction. Our repair process addresses the cause, not just the visible damage:

1. **Diagnosis** — We identify the failure mode: drainage, foundation, structural, or a combination.
2. **Drainage correction** — In most cases, we install or repair French drains and weep systems behind the wall to relieve hydrostatic pressure.
3. **Structural repair or rebuild** — Depending on severity, we may reinforce the existing wall, rebuild failed sections, or replace the entire structure.
4. **Backfill and grading** — Proper backfill material and surface grading prevent future water problems.

## DIY Walls and Previous Bad Work

A large portion of our repair work involves walls that were built without proper engineering — DIY projects, handyman work, or contractors who skipped drainage. If someone stacked blocks or rocks without a compacted base, drain rock, or perforated pipe, the wall was always going to fail. We see it constantly across Northern Utah.
