## Retaining Walls for Commercial and Municipal Projects

Commercial retaining wall projects demand a different level of capability than residential work. Larger walls, engineered specifications, tighter timelines, coordination with other trades, and compliance with municipal codes — these projects need a contractor with heavy equipment, experienced operators, and a track record of delivering on schedule.

Ogden Rock Walls, a division of AccuRite Excavation & Hauling, has served commercial, residential, and government contracts across Northern Utah for over 16 years. We have the equipment fleet, the crew, and the project management experience to handle large-scale retaining wall construction.

## Commercial Services

- **Site retention systems** — Large boulder and block walls for commercial developments, multi-family housing, and retail properties.
- **Road and highway walls** — Retaining structures for road cuts, highway embankments, and bridge abutments.
- **Parking lot retention** — Walls that create usable parking areas on sloped commercial lots.
- **Stormwater management** — Retention and detention systems integrated with retaining wall construction.
- **Slope stabilization** — Large-scale erosion control and hillside stabilization for development sites.
- **Demolition and removal** — Tearing out failed or outdated retaining structures before new construction.
